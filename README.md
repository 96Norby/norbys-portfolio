Build a personal portfolio website for Norbert Reyes, an operations leader at

Vancouver International Airport (YVR) who is transitioning into data analytics.

The site's one job: get him hired as a data analyst. Audience: hiring managers

and recruiters who skim fast, mostly on phones.



STRUCTURE — a single-page site with smooth scrolling and a sticky nav

(links: About / Projects / Resume / Contact):



1. **Hero** — full viewport, name, headline, subline, location, two buttons.

2. **About** — bio paragraph plus three focus cards.

3. **Projects** — three project cards in a grid.

4. **Resume** — work history timeline plus certifications.

5. **Contact** — email and LinkedIn, one warm line.

6. **Footer** — `© 2026 Norbert Reyes · norby.ca`



DESIGN:

- Modern, clean, professional. Data-analyst energy: crisp typography, generous

  whitespace, subtle data-inspired accents (thin grid lines, small chart-like

  motifs — tasteful, never gimmicky).

- Dark theme with one confident accent colour (teal or amber — pick whichever

  looks sharper).

- Must look excellent on mobile. Recruiters browse on phones.

- No stock photos of handshakes, skylines, or winding roads. No lorem ipsum

  anywhere. Every button and link must work — no dead links, no empty sections.
